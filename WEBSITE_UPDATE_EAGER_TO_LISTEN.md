### 📻 Radio U: A Queue That Programs Itself

The heart of the app is the **Curated Airwaves™** engine. I call it **Radio U**. You tell it how much each show matters and how each one should play. It builds the lineup.

* **Priority 1 to 5**: Your must-hear shows move to the front. Weekend banter waits its turn.
* **A play order for every show**:
  * *Serials and courses* play oldest first, so the story makes sense.
  * *Daily News* keeps a rolling window (3 days by default) and automatically clears out anything expired.
  * *Evergreen shows* interleave new episodes with the back catalog.
* **Anti-Binge Diversity Limit**: 1 to 5 episodes per show in a row. Shows take turns, just like a public radio station rotates its hosts.
* **Hot List**: One tap instantly plays every unplayed episode from your Priority 1 shows.
* **Play Next**: Bump any single episode to the front; your curated lineup resumes right after.

### 🚗 Made for the Drive

A commute is where most podcast listening happens. So in-car listening got special attention:

* **Android Auto**: Full dashboard interface featuring your Pending Playlist (with 3–5 upcoming tracks visible), Hot List, Daily News, and Podcasts.
* **Steering Wheel & Headset Controls**: Next/Previous track navigation plus dedicated 90s/15s seeking buttons keep your eyes safely on the road.
* **Clear Offline Status**: Instantly see which tracks are saved on-device (`💾`) so you know you're covered even with zero cell reception.
* **Deep 180s Audio Buffer**: Loads up to three minutes ahead so tunnels and cellular dead zones won't interrupt your audio.
* **Hands-Free**: Ask Google Assistant to find and play a show by voice.
