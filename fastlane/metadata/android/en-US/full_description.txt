Eager to Listen plays your podcasts the way a good radio station would. You tell it which shows matter most, and it builds your day's listening for you: the important stuff first, a fair mix of everything else, and no single show taking over the queue.

<b>Radio U, your own station</b>
• Give each show a priority from P1 to P5. Daily news and must-hear shows go first; weekend listening waits its turn.
• Fair rotation mixes your shows so one prolific feed can't bury the rest.
• Hot List: every unplayed episode from your P1 shows, one tap away.
• Four play orders per show: newest first, oldest first (for serials and courses), news (old episodes drop off after a few days) and evergreen (works through the back catalog).
• Pin an episode to the top and it stays put when the queue rebuilds.
• Per-show playback speed and intro/outro skipping.

<b>Built for the car</b>
• Android Auto: your queue, Hot List, daily news and shows on the car screen.
• Steering-wheel buttons rewind 15 seconds and skip ahead 90.
• Rides out tunnels and dead zones instead of skipping your episode, and recovers when a download goes bad.

<b>Podcasting 2.0</b>
• Transcripts that follow along as you listen; tap a line to jump there.
• Chapters with artwork and links.
• Live episodes, with a LIVE badge.
• Hosts and guests, and the show's own support links.

<b>Finding shows</b>
• Search the open Podcast Index directory (add your free key) and, if you want, Apple Podcasts too. You can turn Apple off in Settings.
• Open podcast and RSS links in the app, or share them to it. You see the show before you subscribe.
• Import from Podcast Addict or OPML; export OPML.

<b>Creator Inspiration Studio</b>
Bookmark the moment you just heard, from the player or the car. Add notes later, share the whole list as Markdown, and keep a permanent archive in your own Google Drive folder with episode links, timestamps and transcript excerpts.

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
• No ads, no tracking, no account.
• Optional sync between your phone and the Eager to Listen desktop app through a folder you pick in your own Google Drive. No server of ours is involved.
• Downloads for offline listening, on Wi-Fi only if you like.

Eager to Listen is in alpha. Bug reports and ideas are welcome on GitHub.
