The Philosophy

Why We Developed Eager to Listen

Modern podcast apps stopped innovating a decade ago. They either treat your queue like a dumb linear playlist or dump entire back-catalogs into an exhausting binge. Here are the core pain points we set out to cure.

🪤

The "Binge Trap" & Queue Monopolization

The Pain Point Subscribing to a 50-episode show dumps the entire catalog in front of everything else. You are trapped listening to one host for 3 weeks or forced to spend 15 minutes manually rearranging items.
Curated Airwaves (Radio U) Solution Enforces Anti-Binge Diversity Limits (1–5 consecutive episodes). The algorithm fairly interleaves your library, providing balanced variety on every commute like a public radio station.
🗞️

Stale Headlines Lingering Forever

The Pain Point Daily morning news shows stay trapped in your playlist for weeks. You end up hearing expired market summaries or obsolete election updates days after they happened.
Curated Airwaves (Radio U) Solution Rolling News Retention Windows automatically discard news older than X days. Plus, Just-In-Time (JIT) Refresh fetches the freshest feed immediately before playback starts!
🔄

Serial vs. Episodic Order Chaos

The Pain Point Mainstream players apply one global rule. If you set "newest first", investigative serials and language courses play backward and ruin the plot.
Curated Airwaves (Radio U) Solution 4 Autonomous Play Strategies: Chronological for serials, Reverse-chronological for banter, Rolling Retention for news, and Evergreen for deep dives.
🚗

Commuter Cellular Dropouts & Skips

The Pain Point Weak cellular coverage or driving through tunnels causes playback to drop, buffer endlessly, or erroneously skip to the next podcast and lose your place.
Curated Airwaves (Radio U) Solution 180-Second Deep Buffer & Reconnect Guard: Holds up to 3 minutes of audio ahead with a 180s back-buffer, allowing 2 track skips with zero network dependency.
📜

Trapped in Flat "Podcasting 1.0" Audio

The Pain Point Standard apps treat podcasts as dumb MP3 files: no clickable transcripts, no chapter art, no live stream detection, and no direct creator funding.
Curated Airwaves (Radio U) Solution Full Podcasting 2.0 Engine: Interactive word-highlighted transcripts with tap-to-seek, rich chapters with artwork, live broadcast feeds, and Lightning satoshi Boostagrams.
🔒

Proprietary Server Surveillance

The Pain Point Apps force you to create proprietary accounts so they can track your listening habits, inject targeted ads, and sell behavioral telemetry.
Curated Airwaves (Radio U) Solution Zero-Server Privacy: Syncs your queue, preferences, and subscriptions directly using your private Google Drive AppData folder. No tracking, no middleman server.

Fine-Grained Anti-Binge Rules & Automation

Full control over how your personal radio station behaves. Adjust consecutive episode caps, rolling news retention windows, scheduled updates, and offline pre-downloading.
