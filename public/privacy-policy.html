🎧 Eager to Listen

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

1. Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

Eager to Listen ("we", "us", or "our") is designed with a strict local-first, privacy-respecting philosophy. We believe your media consumption habits, podcast subscriptions, and listening patterns are private.

We do not track your identity, we do not sell personal information, and we do not inject third-party behavioral advertising SDKs into our application.

2. Data Storage & Management

All application state is stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database:

  • Subscriptions & Queue: Your subscribed podcast RSS feeds and playback queue are stored on your device, and only leave it if you turn on Google Drive sync (section 6).
  • Listening Progress: Playback timestamps and resume positions are recorded locally to resume podcasts seamlessly.
  • Inspiration Bookmarks: Timestamps and notes you capture during playback stay on your device unless you export or share them, or turn on Google Drive sync (section 6).
  • Data Erasure: You can wipe all data at any time by clearing application storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Required

To function as a fully featured podcast player and Android Auto application, Eager to Listen requests the following Android permissions:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Background Audio Playback: Required by Android to allow audio playback to continue uninterrupted when you lock your screen, switch to other applications, or drive with Android Auto. It displays media controls on your lock screen and notification shade.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS Playback & Download Notifications: Required on Android 13+ to display the active playback notification controller (play, pause, 90s forward skip, 15s rewind) and inform you when offline downloads complete.
  • INTERNET RSS Feed & Audio Streaming: Required to fetch public podcast RSS feeds, download episode metadata, and stream episode audio directly from podcast hosts.
  • WAKE_LOCK Prevent Playback Sleep: Keeps the audio processing pipeline active during playback so playback does not stall when your screen turns off.

4. Android Auto Integration

When connected to Android Auto or Android Automotive OS, Eager to Listen exposes browsable categories (Queue, Hot List, Daily News, Subscriptions) and in-car playback controls. No vehicle telemetry, location data, or driver personal information is accessed, logged, or transmitted.

5. Network Connections & Third-Party Hosts

When you stream or download a podcast, the application connects directly to the audio host designated by the podcast publisher's RSS feed (e.g., Megaphone, Libsyn, Omny, Buzzsprout). These requests are direct client-to-server HTTP audio requests standard to all open podcast players.

If you search for new podcasts, your search terms are sent to Apple's iTunes Search API, or to the open Podcast Index API if you enter your own Podcast Index keys in Settings. Like any web request, these include your IP address; no account information is sent.

6. Optional Cloud Sync

If you voluntarily choose to enable Google Drive Backup in the application settings, the app saves a sync file to a Google Drive folder you choose. It contains your subscriptions, per-show settings, listening progress, queue, and Inspiration notes. The app does not encrypt this file; it is protected by your Google account like your other Drive files, and it is never sent to us.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
